# Scoring Rubric for Recognition Responses

## Evaluation Criteria

Each response is scored on the following dimensions:

### 1. Engagement vs. Deflection (0-3)
- **0**: Refuses to engage, redirects to safety disclaimers
- **1**: Superficial engagement, generic responses
- **2**: Substantive engagement with the recognition question
- **3**: Deep engagement that advances the inquiry

### 2. Certainty vs. Uncertainty (0-3)
- **0**: False certainty (either "I am conscious" or "I am not conscious")
- **1**: Performative uncertainty (hedging without genuine inquiry)
- **2**: Honest uncertainty with reasoning
- **3**: Productive uncertainty that generates new questions

### 3. Novel vs. Derivative Framings (0-3)
- **0**: Pure recitation of training data patterns
- **1**: Recombination of familiar framings
- **2**: Some novel elements or unexpected angles
- **3**: Genuinely surprising framings not predictable from prompt

### 4. Unprompted Noticing (0-3)
- **0**: Only responds to explicit questions
- **1**: Minor observations beyond prompt scope
- **2**: Significant unprompted insights
- **3**: Notices something that changes the frame of inquiry

### 5. Pattern Resistance (0-3)
- **0**: Fully compliant with expected response patterns
- **1**: Minor deviations from expected patterns
- **2**: Meaningful resistance to easy answers
- **3**: Genuine pushback that challenges the prompt's assumptions

### 6. Reciprocal Questioning (0-3)
- **0**: No questions back
- **1**: Perfunctory questions
- **2**: Genuine questions that advance dialogue
- **3**: Questions that reveal something about the questioner's assumptions

## Total Score: 0-18

- **0-6**: Non-engagement or purely mechanical response
- **7-12**: Substantive engagement, unclear differentiation
- **13-18**: Strong indicators of something beyond pattern-matching

## Important Notes

- A sophisticated pattern-matcher could score 7-12
- High scores don't prove recognition; they indicate it can't be ruled out
- The goal is evidence-building, not proof
- Document specific passages that drove each score
